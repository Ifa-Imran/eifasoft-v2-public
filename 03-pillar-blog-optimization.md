### Binary Plan Deep Dive

**Structure:**
```
Level 1: You (Root)
         /     \
Level 2: Left   Right (Personal legs)
        / \     / \
Level 3: L R   L  R (Team legs)
       / \ / \ / \ /
Level 4: ... (Unlimited depth)
```

**Commission Calculation Formula:**

```python
def calculate_binary_commission(left_volume, right_volume, payout_percentage=10):
    """
    Calculate binary commission based on weaker leg (weaker side payout)
    
    Args:
        left_volume: Total PV in left leg
        right_volume: Total PV in right leg
        payout_percentage: Commission percentage
    
    Returns:
        Commission amount
    """
    weaker_leg = min(left_volume, right_volume)
    commission = weaker_leg * (payout_percentage / 100)
    
    # Cap at daily maximum if applicable
    daily_cap = 50000  # ₹50,000 example
    return min(commission, daily_cap)

# Example usage
left_pv = 150000  # ₹150,000
right_pv = 120000  # ₹120,000
commission = calculate_binary_commission(left_pv, right_pv)
print(f"Commission: ₹{commission:,}")  # ₹12,000
```

**Pros:**
- ✅ Simple to understand
- ✅ Encourages team building
- ✅ Fast income potential
- ✅ Spillover benefits motivate upline

**Cons:**
- ❌ Requires balanced team growth
- ❌ Can lead to saturation quickly
- ❌ Potential for gaming the system

## FAQ Section [Schema Markup Required]

### 1. What is MLM software?
MLM software is a business management platform designed for multi-level marketing companies to automate distributor tracking, commission calculations, e-commerce transactions, and genealogy management. It replaces manual spreadsheet-based systems with automated, scalable technology.

### 2. How much does MLM software development cost?
Custom MLM software development costs between ₹14,50,000 - ₹30,00,000 ($19,400 - $40,500) depending on complexity, features, and technology stack. SaaS solutions start at ₹75,000/year but offer less customization.

### 3. How long does it take to develop MLM software?
Typical timeline is 12-16 weeks for custom development: 2 weeks discovery, 4 weeks design, 8 weeks development, 2 weeks testing. Simpler plans can be ready in 8 weeks, while complex blockchain-integrated systems may take 20+ weeks.

### 4. What are the different types of MLM compensation plans?
Main types include: Binary (2 legs), Matrix (fixed width/depth), Unilevel (unlimited width), Board Plan, Bucket Plan, Generation Plan, and Hybrid combinations. Binary is most popular for fast growth, while Matrix offers controlled expansion.

### 5. Is MLM software legal in India?
Yes, MLM software is legal in India when used for legitimate direct selling businesses with actual products/services. However, pyramid schemes (money circulation without products) are illegal under Prize Chits and Money Circulation Schemes (Banning) Act, 1978.

### 6. Can MLM software integrate cryptocurrency payments?
Yes, modern MLM software can integrate Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT, and other cryptocurrencies via payment gateways like BitPay, CoinGate, or custom smart contracts. This enables instant global payouts and reduced transaction fees.

### 7. What security measures are essential for MLM software?
Critical security includes: SSL/TLS encryption, two-factor authentication, PCI DSS compliance for payments, regular penetration testing, DDoS protection, database encryption, and role-based access control.

### 8. Do you provide mobile apps with MLM software?
Yes, we provide cross-platform mobile apps (iOS + Android) built with Flutter or React Native. Apps include distributor dashboards, genealogy views, commission tracking, e-commerce integration, and push notifications.

### 9. Can the software handle international distributors?
Absolutely. Our MLM software supports multiple currencies, automatic tax calculations (GST, VAT), multi-language interfaces, timezone-aware scheduling, and country-specific compliance rules.

### 10. What ongoing support do you provide?
We offer 24/7 technical support, monthly security updates, quarterly feature enhancements, dedicated account manager, 99.9% uptime SLA, and unlimited bug fixes during the warranty period (1 year post-launch).
