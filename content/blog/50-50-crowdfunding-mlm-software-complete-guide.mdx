The 50-50 Crowdfunding MLM Plan combines direct peer-to-peer contributions (50%) with a matrix structure (50%). Members help each other while building a team. This model is popular in India for its dual-benefit structure.

## How 50-50 Crowdfunding MLM Works
- Member joins at an entry amount
- 50% goes directly to their sponsor (direct help)
- 50% goes into a matrix pool distributed upline
- Member earns when their own recruits join and contribute

## Key Features
- Dual commission engine (direct + matrix)
- Real-time matrix position tracking
- Sponsor direct payment automation
- E-wallet for matrix earnings
- UPI and bank transfer integration

## Why This Plan Works in India
The 50-50 model resonates with Indian audiences because the direct help component creates immediate, tangible value. Members see real money flowing to their sponsor, building trust in the system.
