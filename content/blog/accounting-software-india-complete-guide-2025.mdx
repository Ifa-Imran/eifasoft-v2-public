# Accounting Software India: Complete Guide for Small & Mid-Size Businesses (2025)

Every Indian business — whether a startup, a trading company, or a manufacturing unit — needs accounting software that handles **GST compliance, invoicing, inventory, payroll, and financial reporting** without complexity or excessive cost.

The market offers everything from Tally ERP to QuickBooks to custom-built solutions. Choosing the wrong one creates compliance risks, data silos, and operational bottlenecks.

This guide helps you understand what accounting software should do, what options exist for Indian businesses, and when custom development makes more sense than off-the-shelf products.

At [Eifasoft Technologies](/erp-software-solutions-complete-guide-india-2025), we build custom accounting modules and ERP systems that match the specific workflows of Indian businesses — from retail chains to manufacturing units to service firms.

## What Should Accounting Software Do for an Indian Business?

### Mandatory Capabilities
- **GST invoicing** — B2B and B2C with correct CGST/SGST/IGST calculation
- **GST return filing** — GSTR-1, GSTR-3B data preparation
- **E-invoicing** — IRN generation for businesses with turnover > ₹5 crore
- **E-way bill** — generation for goods transport
- **TDS/TCS management** — deduction tracking and return data
- **Bank reconciliation** — import bank statements and match entries

### Core Financial Features
- Chart of accounts and ledger management
- Purchase and sales voucher entry
- Debit/credit note management
- Accounts payable and receivable tracking
- Cash and cheque payment management
- Financial statements — P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow

### Advanced Capabilities (for growing businesses)
- Inventory management with FIFO/LIFO/Weighted Average
- Multi-location stock tracking
- Manufacturing and job costing
- Project/cost centre accounting
- Payroll with PF, ESIC, PT, TDS
- Multi-currency transactions
- Budget management and variance analysis

## Top Accounting Software Options for Indian Businesses

### Tally ERP 9 / Tally Prime
**Best for**: Small businesses, traders, retailers
- Widely used, CA-friendly
- Strong GST compliance
- Limited collaboration/cloud access
- ₹18,000–54,000 one-time + AMC

### Zoho Books
**Best for**: Service businesses, startups
- Cloud-based, excellent UI
- GST-compliant with e-invoicing
- Good invoicing, weaker inventory
- ₹2,500–7,500/month

### QuickBooks India
**Best for**: Service firms, freelancers
- Easy to use
- Basic GST support
- Limited Indian-specific features
- ₹3,000–7,000/month

### Busy Accounting Software
**Best for**: Trading and manufacturing
- Strong inventory features
- Good GST compliance
- ₹9,000–27,000/year

- Fully customized to your processes
- Integrated with your CRM, inventory, POS, or HR
- No per-user limits

## When Does Custom Accounting Software Make Sense?

Custom development becomes justified when:

1. **Standard software can't match your workflow** — e.g., project-based billing, franchise accounting, industry-specific costing
2. **Integration is critical** — your accounting needs to sync live with a POS, e-commerce store, or industry ERP
3. **Multi-entity management** — managing multiple companies or branches in one platform
4. **Scalability concerns** — per-user costs becoming prohibitive as you grow
5. **Data ownership** — regulated industries needing complete data control
