## The 5 Reasons Enterprise AI Fails

1. **Wrong use case selection**: Starting with impressive demos rather than high-ROI business problems
2. **Poor data foundation**: Attempting ML with insufficient, dirty, or siloed data
3. **No clear ownership**: AI projects with no accountable business sponsor
4. **Underestimating change management**: Tools deployed but not adopted
5. **Build vs buy confusion**: Custom development where proven vendor solutions exist

## FAQ

**Q: Do we need an AI strategy before starting development?**
For quick wins and standard use cases (chatbot, automation) — no, start now. For custom model development, data platform investment, or enterprise-wide AI — yes, strategy first prevents costly missteps.

**Q: How is AI consulting different from data consulting?**
Data consulting focuses on data architecture and analytics. AI consulting covers data + model strategy + business transformation + governance. Eifasoft does both.

**Q: Can small businesses (under 100 employees) benefit from AI consulting?**
Yes — SMB AI consulting focuses on automation quick wins and off-the-shelf AI tools rather than custom model development. Budget ₹50,000–₹1,50,000 for an SMB AI strategy engagement.
