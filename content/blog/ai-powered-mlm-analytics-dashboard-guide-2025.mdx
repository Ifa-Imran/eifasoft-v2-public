## Core AI Analytics Modules

### 1. Churn Prediction Engine
Using machine learning (gradient boosting / LSTM models), the system analyzes behavioral patterns to identify distributors at high churn risk:
- Login frequency decline
- Commission drop below historical average
- No product purchases in 30 days
- Reduced recruitment activity
- Support ticket volume spike

**Output**: Risk score (0–100) per member with recommended retention action.

### 2. Network Growth Velocity Tracker
Visualizes real-time network expansion with velocity metrics:
- New member joining rate (daily/weekly trend)
- Leg growth ratio for binary networks
- Geographic heat map of network density
- Depth growth rate by tree branch

### 3. Revenue Forecasting
ML models predict next 30/60/90 day revenue based on:
- Historical commission cycle patterns
- Current recruitment pipeline
- Product sales trends
- Seasonal adjustment factors

Accuracy rate: 85–92% for networks with 6+ months of data.

### 4. Top Performer Intelligence
Automatically identifies and profiles your top earners:
- Recruitment conversion rate
- Team retention rate
- Product sales volume
- Network building speed
- Replication factor (how well their team duplicates their success)

**Use case**: Identify your "super-connectors" and replicate their strategies through AI-generated training recommendations.

### 5. Compliance & Fraud Detection
AI monitors transaction patterns to detect:
- Circular commission chains (regulatory red flag)
- Self-enrollment loops
- Unusual withdrawal patterns (money laundering indicators)
- Bot/fake account detection


## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How much training data is needed for accurate predictions?**
Minimum 3 months of data for basic predictions; 12+ months for high-accuracy revenue forecasting. The models improve continuously as more data accumulates.

**Q: Can AI analytics be added to existing MLM software?**
Yes — as long as your MLM database is accessible (MySQL/PostgreSQL/MongoDB), Eifasoft can build an analytics layer on top without modifying your core application.

**Q: Does this work for small networks (under 1,000 members)?**
Basic analytics yes; predictive ML requires sufficient data volume. For small networks, we configure rule-based alerts instead of ML models until sufficient data exists.
