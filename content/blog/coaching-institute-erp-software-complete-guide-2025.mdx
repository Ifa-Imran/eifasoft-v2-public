# Coaching Institute ERP Software: Complete Guide for Education Centers (2025)

Running a coaching institute today means managing hundreds of students across multiple batches, tracking fees, scheduling faculty, conducting online and offline classes — all simultaneously. Without proper software, even a 200-student institute creates administrative chaos.

**Coaching Institute ERP software** centralizes every operation: admissions, fees, attendance, academics, communication, and performance analytics — giving institute owners full visibility and control.

At [Eifasoft Technologies](/erp-software-solutions-complete-guide-india-2025), we build custom coaching institute management systems used by IIT-JEE coaching centers, UPSC academies, competitive exam institutes, and after-school tuition chains across India.

---

## Key Modules in Coaching Institute ERP

### 1. Admissions & Enrollment Management
- Online inquiry capture from website/social media
- Lead pipeline with follow-up reminders
- Admission form and document collection
- Enrollment confirmation with welcome kit automation
- Batch allotment based on subject/level

### 2. Batch & Schedule Management
- Multi-batch creation (morning/evening/weekend/online)
- Subject-wise faculty assignment
- Timetable builder with conflict detection
- Batch capacity management
- Batch transfer/upgrade workflows

### 3. Fee Management & Collection
- Fee structure configuration (monthly/quarterly/annual)
- Scholarship and discount management
- Automated fee reminders via WhatsApp/SMS
- Online payment integration (UPI, Razorpay, Paytm)
- Receipt generation with GST compliance
- Defaulter reports and collection analytics
- Instalment plan management

### 4. Attendance Tracking
- Biometric/RFID attendance for students
- Faculty attendance management
- Parent notifications for absentees (WhatsApp/SMS)
- Attendance reports by batch, subject, date range
- Low-attendance alerts for counseling

### 5. Academic Management
- Syllabus mapping by batch/subject
- Lecture completion tracking
- Test and assignment scheduling
- Answer sheet management (OMR/manual)
- Marks entry and report card generation

### 6. Performance Analytics
- Student performance dashboard
- Rank tracking across internal tests
- Weak area identification per student
- Comparative performance reports (batch vs. batch)
- Parent progress sharing via portal or WhatsApp

### 7. Online Learning Management
- Live class scheduling (Zoom/Google Meet integration)
- Recorded video upload and access control
- Online test portal with auto-evaluation
- Study material (PDF/video) distribution
- Discussion forum and doubt submission

### 8. Faculty Management
- Faculty profile and subject expertise mapping
- Lecture assignment and completion tracking
- Salary processing based on hours/sessions
- Performance reviews linked to student outcomes

### 9. Communication Module
- Bulk SMS/WhatsApp for announcements
- Fee reminders and admission notifications
- Parent portal with student progress view
- Student mobile app for schedule, results, materials

### 10. Accounts & Reporting
- Income and expense tracking
- Branch-wise P&L reports
- GST return preparation data
- Custom MIS reports for management

## Technology Stack

- **Backend**: Node.js or Laravel (PHP) — fast, scalable
- **Frontend**: React.js — modern, role-based dashboards
- **Mobile Apps**: Flutter — single codebase for Android + iOS (student and parent apps)
- **Database**: MySQL/PostgreSQL — structured fee and academic data
- **Payments**: Razorpay, PayU, PayTM integration
- **Communication**: MSG91 (SMS), Interakt (WhatsApp Business API)
- **Cloud**: AWS / DigitalOcean
