## Why Industry-Specific ERP?

Generic ERP systems like SAP, Oracle, and Tally are designed for broad use cases. They require expensive customization to fit specific industry workflows. Custom-built ERP offers:

- **Perfect process fit** — built around your specific workflow, not retrofitted
- **Lower total cost** — no expensive license fees or consultant customization charges
- **Faster adoption** — intuitive UI that matches staff mental models
- **Local compliance** — GST, TDS, Aadhaar, CBSE/ICSE standards, NABH norms
- **Mobile-first** — staff apps for all roles, not just admin desktops
- **Integration flexibility** — connect with payment gateways, SMS, WhatsApp, biometrics

## FAQ Section

### 1. Does Eifasoft provide cloud-based or on-premise ERP?
Both options are available. Cloud-based ERP is recommended for lower infrastructure cost and automatic updates. On-premise is available for institutions with strict data privacy requirements.

### 2. Can your ERP handle GST invoicing and compliance?
Yes. All Eifasoft ERP modules include GST-compliant invoicing with automatic GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B data preparation, e-invoice (IRN), and e-way bill generation.

### 3. Is there a mobile app for students/patients/staff?
Yes. Every ERP product includes dedicated mobile apps for each user role — patients/students/parents and staff/doctors/teachers — built with Flutter for iOS and Android.

### 4. Can your hospital software integrate with Ayushman Bharat (PMJAY)?
Yes. Eifasoft's hospital management software includes ABHA integration, PMJAY claim processing, and NABH compliance reports.

### 5. How does Eifasoft handle data migration from existing systems?
We provide structured data migration from Excel, Tally, legacy databases, and other ERP systems. Our migration team handles data cleaning, transformation, and validation.
