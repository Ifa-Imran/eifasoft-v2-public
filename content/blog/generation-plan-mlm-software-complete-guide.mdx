## What is Generation Plan MLM?

**Generation Plan MLM** is a compensation model where leaders earn commission overrides from entire "generations" of distributors in their organization. A generation is defined as a group of distributors between leadership ranks (e.g., all distributors between your rank and the next leader's rank).

### Key Characteristics:
- **Generation-Based Overrides**: Earn from entire generations, not just levels
- **Leadership Focus**: Rewards building leaders, not just recruits
- **Deep Passive Income**: Overrides can extend 4-8 generations deep
- **Scalable Earnings**: No limit on generations or income
- **Team Development**: Incentivizes mentoring and training

## How Generation Overrides Work

### Generation Definition

A **generation** is the group of distributors between two leadership ranks in your organization.

```
You (Diamond Leader)
  ↓
Generation 1: All distributors until next Diamond
  - Includes Gold, Platinum, Silver, Distributors
  ↓
Diamond #1 (breakaway leader)
  ↓
Generation 2: All distributors until next Diamond
  ↓
Diamond #2 (breakaway leader)
  ↓
Generation 3: And so on...
```

### Generation Override Example

```
You are a Diamond leader earning 10% generation overrides.

Your Organization:
- Generation 1: 50 distributors doing $50,000 PV/month
  → Your override: 10% × $50,000 = $5,000
  
- Generation 2: Diamond leader + 40 distributors ($40,000 PV)
  → Your override: 10% × $40,000 = $4,000
  
- Generation 3: Diamond leader + 35 distributors ($35,000 PV)
  → Your override: 10% × $35,000 = $3,500
  
Total Monthly Generation Overrides: $12,500
```

## Commission Structure & Formulas

### Generation Override Formula

```
Generation Override = Override % × Generation Group PV

Where:
- Override % = Commission percentage for that generation
- Generation Group PV = Total product volume in that generation

Example (Diamond Leader with 10% overrides):
Gen 1: 10% × $60,000 = $6,000
Gen 2: 10% × $45,000 = $4,500
Gen 3: 7% × $35,000 = $2,450
Gen 4: 7% × $28,000 = $1,960
Gen 5: 5% × $20,000 = $1,000
Gen 6: 5% × $15,000 = $750
Total Monthly: $16,660
```

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a generation in MLM?
A generation in MLM is a group of distributors between two leadership ranks in your organization. For example, if you're a Diamond leader, Generation 1 includes all distributors from your direct recruits until the next Diamond leader. Generation 2 starts from that Diamond and continues until the next Diamond, and so on. You earn commission overrides from each generation.

### How does generation override work?
Generation overrides work by paying leaders a percentage (typically 10-15%) of the total product volume generated by each generation in their organization. For example, if you're a Diamond leader with 10% overrides and Generation 1 produces $50,000 in PV, you earn $5,000 from that generation. If you have 6 generations, you earn from all 6, creating deep passive income.

### What's the difference between generation plan and stair step plan?
**Generation Plan**: Pays overrides based on generations (groups between leaders), focuses on building leaders, extends 4-8 generations deep, creates passive income.

**Stair Step Plan**: Pays based on rank advancement, includes breakaway mechanism, focuses on rank progression, commissions increase with each rank.

Generation plans reward leader development; stair step plans reward individual rank achievement.

### How much can you earn with generation plan MLM?
Leader earnings vary by generations and PV:
- **1-2 generations**: $1,000-$3,000/month
- **3-4 generations**: $3,000-$8,000/month

- **7-8 generations**: $15,000-$25,000+/month

Top leaders (Crown rank with 8 generations) typically earn $15,000-$25,000/month in passive overrides.
