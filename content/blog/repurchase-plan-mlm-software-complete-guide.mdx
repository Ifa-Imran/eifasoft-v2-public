## What is Repurchase Plan MLM?

**Repurchase Plan MLM** is a compensation model where distributors earn commissions not just on initial sales, but on every repeat purchase made by their downline. This creates predictable, recurring revenue streams for both the company and distributors.

### Key Characteristics:
- **Recurring Commissions**: Earn on every repurchase cycle (monthly, quarterly)
- **Subscription-Based**: Members maintain active status through regular purchases
- **Product-Focused**: Revenue driven by actual product consumption
- **Passive Income**: Build residual income from team's ongoing purchases
- **High Retention**: 70-85% retention vs. 40-50% traditional MLM

## How Repurchase Commissions Work

### The Repurchase Cycle

```
Month 1: Customer joins & purchases ($100)
    ↓ Earn 20% direct commission = $20
    
Month 2: Customer repurchases ($100)
    ↓ Earn 10% repurchase commission = $10
    
Month 3-12: Customer continues repurchasing
    ↓ Earn 10% each month = $100 total
    
Year 1 Total from 1 Customer: $120 in commissions
```

### Commission Structure by Level

| Level | Commission Rate | Description |
|-------|----------------|-------------|
| **Level 1 (Direct)** | 15-25% | Personally sponsored members |
| **Level 2** | 8-12% | Second level downline |
| **Level 3** | 5-7% | Third level downline |
| **Levels 4-6** | 2-4% | Deeper levels |
| **Levels 7-10** | 1-2% | Residual income |

**Example Calculation:**
```
100 active subscribers × $100/month = $10,000 monthly PV

Level 1: 20% × $2,000 PV (20 direct) = $400
Level 2: 10% × $3,000 PV = $300
Level 3: 7% × $2,500 PV = $175
Level 4-6: 3% × $2,500 PV = $75
Total Monthly Recurring Commission: $950
Annual Recurring Income: $11,400
```

### Active Status Requirements

To earn repurchase commissions, distributors must maintain active status:

1. **Personal Repurchase**: Monthly minimum purchase ($50-$150)
2. **Active Downline**: Minimum 3-5 active recruits
3. **Purchase Frequency**: Every 30-45 days
4. **Grace Period**: 1-2 months before deactivation

**Inactive = No Commissions**: If a distributor stops repurchasing, they don't earn commissions from their downline's repurchases (but their upline still earns).

Where:
- Level Rate = Commission % for specific depth level

## Payment & Billing Automation

### Billing Cycle Management

**Monthly Billing Flow:**
```
Day 1-5: Process subscriptions (staggered by signup date)
Day 6-10: Retry failed payments (3 attempts)
Day 11-15: Send payment failure notifications
Day 16-20: Grace period (subscriptions remain active)
Day 21-25: Deactivate unpaid subscriptions
Day 26-30: Generate reports, prepare next cycle
```

### Failed Payment Handling

**Retry Strategy:**
```
Attempt 1: Billing date (immediate retry)
Attempt 2: +3 days (send email notification)
Attempt 3: +7 days (send SMS + email)
Final: +14 days (deactivate, start win-back campaign)

Success Rate by Attempt:
- Attempt 1: 75%
- Attempt 2: 15%
- Attempt 3: 6%
- Total Recovery: 96% of failed payments
```

### Payment Security

1. **PCI DSS Compliance**
   - Tokenized payment data (no raw card numbers stored)
   - Encrypted transmission (TLS 1.3)
   - Regular security audits (quarterly)

2. **Fraud Prevention**
   - Velocity checks (max 3 subscriptions per card)
   - Address verification (AVS)
   - CVV validation
   - 3D Secure authentication (high-value orders)

3. **Chargeback Protection**
   - Clear billing descriptors on statements
   - Easy cancellation process (reduces friendly fraud)
   - Detailed transaction records for disputes
   - Chargeback alert services (Early Warning System)

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

### 1. No Auto-Ship Incentives
**Problem:** Customers cancel after first purchase.  
**Solution:** Offer 10-15% discount for auto-ship subscriptions.  
**Impact:** +35% retention rate.

### 2. Poor Failed Payment Handling
**Problem:** 25% of failed payments never recovered.  
**Solution:** Implement 3-attempt retry logic over 14 days.  
**Impact:** 96% payment recovery rate.

### 3. Complex Cancellation Process
**Problem:** Customers dispute charges (friendly fraud).  
**Solution:** One-click cancellation, no phone calls required.  
**Impact:** 60% reduction in chargebacks.

### 4. Ignoring Inactive Subscribers
**Problem:** 40% churn within first 90 days.  
**Solution:** Automated win-back campaigns at 30/60/90 days.  
**Impact:** 45% recovery rate for 30-day inactive.

### 5. Unclear Active Status Requirements
**Problem:** Distributors confused about earning commissions.  
**Solution:** Dashboard shows active status, countdown to next purchase.  
**Impact:** 80% reduction in support tickets.

### 6. Overpaying Commissions
**Problem:** Total payout exceeds 55% of revenue.  
**Solution:** Cap recurring commissions at 40-50% of subscription value.  
**Monitoring:** Weekly payout ratio reports.

### 7. No Product Consumption Tracking
**Problem:** Customers hoard products, delay repurchases.  
**Solution:** Smart reminders based on product usage rates.  
**Example:** 30-day supply → reminder on day 25.

### 8. Inadequate Testing
**Problem:** Billing errors discovered after launch.  
**Solution:** Test with 1,000+ simulated subscriptions before go-live.  
**Accuracy Target:** 99.9% billing and commission accuracy.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a repurchase plan in MLM?
A repurchase plan in MLM is a compensation model where distributors earn recurring commissions from repeat product purchases made by their downline. Instead of one-time commissions on initial sales, distributors build passive income from their team's ongoing monthly or quarterly purchases. This model aligns company revenue with actual product consumption, creating sustainable growth.

### How does auto-ship work in MLM?
Auto-ship automatically charges a customer's payment method and ships products on a recurring schedule (usually monthly). Customers subscribe to receive specific products at set intervals. The MLM software calculates and distributes commissions to upline distributors on each auto-ship order. Customers can modify, pause, or cancel subscriptions at any time through their dashboard.

### What's the average retention rate for repurchase plan MLMs?
Successful repurchase plan MLMs achieve **70-85% retention rates**, compared to 40-50% for traditional MLMs. Key retention drivers include auto-ship discounts (10-15%), free shipping, loyalty programs, and proactive churn prevention campaigns. Health supplements typically see 75-80% retention, while digital products achieve 80-85%.

### How much does repurchase plan MLM software cost?
Custom repurchase plan MLM software costs **$25,000-$80,000** for development, plus $3,000-$7,000/month for hosting, payment processing, and support. Costs depend on:
- Subscription management complexity
- Number of payment gateways
- Email/SMS automation requirements
- Custom commission rules
- Expected subscriber volume (1K vs. 50K+)

### What commission percentages should I set for repurchases?
Recommended repurchase commission structure:
- **Level 1**: 15-20% (direct recruits)
- **Level 2**: 8-12%
- **Level 3**: 5-7%
- **Levels 4-6**: 2-4%
- **Levels 7-10**: 1-2%
- **Total**: 35-50% maximum payout

Lower than initial purchase commissions (which are typically 25-35% for Level 1) to maintain profitability on recurring revenue.

### How do I prevent churn in subscription-based MLM?
Implement these retention strategies:
1. **Auto-ship discounts**: 10-15% off for subscribers
2. **Proactive communication**: Reminders before billing
3. **Failed payment recovery**: 3-attempt retry logic
4. **Win-back campaigns**: Targeted offers at 30/60/90 days inactive
5. **Loyalty rewards**: Points, badges, exclusive products
6. **Easy cancellation**: Reduces chargebacks, improves trust
7. **Product satisfaction**: High-quality products that deliver results

### Can repurchase plans work with one-time purchase products?
Repurchase plans work best with consumable products (supplements, skincare, food) that customers use regularly. For one-time purchase products (electronics, furniture), consider hybrid models:
- Offer subscription for consumable accessories
- Create loyalty program for repeat customers
- Implement referral bonuses instead of repurchase commissions

### How long does it take to implement repurchase plan MLM software?
Standard implementation takes **8-12 weeks**:
- **Weeks 1-2**: Planning and requirements
- **Weeks 3-8**: Development (subscription engine, commissions, UI)
- **Weeks 9-10**: Testing and QA
- **Weeks 11-12**: Production launch and training

Complex projects with multiple integrations may take 14-16 weeks.

### What payment methods should I support for subscriptions?
Essential payment methods for MLM subscriptions:
1. **Credit/Debit Cards**: Visa, Mastercard, Amex (90% of customers)
2. **ACH/Bank Transfer**: Lower fees, higher retention (5-10%)
3. **Digital Wallets**: PayPal, Apple Pay (5-8%)
4. **Cryptocurrency**: Optional for tech-savvy markets (1-3%)

Offer at least 2-3 methods to maximize conversion and reduce involuntary churn from declined cards.

### Is repurchase plan MLM legal?
Yes, repurchase plan MLM is legal when properly structured. Compliance requirements:
- Focus on product sales, not recruitment
- No mandatory inventory purchases
- Clear cancellation policy (easy to cancel)
- Income disclosure statements
- FTC compliance (US) or equivalent authority
- Consult MLM attorney before launch
