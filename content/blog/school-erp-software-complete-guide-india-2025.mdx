# School ERP Software: Complete Guide for Indian Schools (2025)

Managing a school in India in 2025 involves navigating CBSE/ICSE/State Board compliance, parental expectations for digital communication, competitive admissions, and complex fee structures — all while running day-to-day academics. **School ERP software** brings every one of these functions into a unified, accessible platform.

At [Eifasoft Technologies](/erp-software-solutions-complete-guide-india-2025), we build custom school management systems for CBSE schools, private institutions, and multi-branch school groups across India.

---

## Core Modules of a School ERP System

### 1. Admissions Management
- Online inquiry and application form
- Document upload and verification
- Merit/lottery/interview scheduling
- Admission confirmation and enrollment
- Fee structure assignment at admission

### 2. Student Information System (SIS)
- Comprehensive student profile (academic, medical, family)
- Transfer certificate and migration certificate generation
- Student ID card printing
- Alumni tracking

### 3. Fee Management
- Flexible fee heads (tuition, transport, activity, lab)
- Concession and scholarship management
- Online fee payment (UPI, Razorpay, net banking)
- Automated reminders via WhatsApp and SMS
- GST-compliant receipts
- Defaulter alerts and reports
- Sibling discounts and staff concessions

### 4. Attendance Management
- Biometric/RFID attendance for students and staff
- Period-wise attendance by subject teacher
- Parent notifications for absent students (real-time WhatsApp)
- Attendance reports for CBSE TC requirements
- Short attendance alerts and counseling triggers

### 5. Academic Management
- Class and section configuration
- Subject and teacher assignment
- Lesson plan submission and approval
- Homework assignment and submission tracking
- Syllabus completion monitoring
- PTM (Parent-Teacher Meeting) scheduling

### 6. Examination Management
- Exam schedule creation and hall ticket generation
- Marks entry (online/offline)
- Automated report card generation (CBSE/ICSE patterns)
- Co-scholastic assessment tracking
- Grade calculation and promotion criteria
- Board exam form management

### 7. Library Management
- Book catalog with barcode/RFID
- Borrowing and return tracking
- Fine calculation for late returns
- Book reservation and renewal
- Acquisition management

### 8. Transport Management
- Route and bus management
- Student-to-route assignment
- GPS tracking integration
- Transport fee collection
- Driver and conductor records

### 9. Hostel Management
- Room and bed allocation
- Mess management and meal tracking
- Hostel fee and visitor management
- Warden communication portal

### 10. HR & Payroll
- Staff profiles with qualification and experience
- Leave management with approval workflows
- Salary processing with PF, ESIC, PT compliance
- Staff attendance with biometric integration

### 11. Parent & Student Portal
- Real-time access to attendance, marks, homework
- Fee payment and receipt download
- School circular and notice board
- Teacher communication
- Mobile app for Android and iOS

### 12. Communication Module
- Bulk SMS/WhatsApp for announcements
- Automated fee reminders
- Exam schedule notifications
- Emergency broadcast system

## Compliance & Board Requirements

Our school ERP is designed for Indian board requirements:

- **CBSE**: TC format, attendance percentage reports, cumulative record sheets
- **ICSE/ISC**: Grade-based reporting, internal assessment tracking
- **State Boards**: Customizable for UP Board, Maharashtra Board, etc.
- **UDISE+**: Data export for annual government reporting
- **RTE**: Free seat management and compliance tracking
