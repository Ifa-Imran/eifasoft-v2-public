## How to Choose a Software Partner in Bangalore

1. **Evaluate domain expertise** — Do they understand your industry's specific needs?
2. **Check technology breadth** — Can they handle your full tech stack requirements?
3. **Review past work** — Request case studies and client references in similar sectors
4. **Understand support model** — Post-delivery SLA, maintenance, and update processes
5. **Verify GST compliance** — Ensure proper tax documentation for your accounts
6. **Assess communication** — Regular status updates, sprint demos, project transparency
