## How Delhi Businesses Choose Software Partners

**Key evaluation criteria**:
1. **Domain expertise** — Does the company understand your industry?
2. **Technology capability** — Can they build what you need (not just what they know)?
3. **Post-delivery support** — Do they offer SLA-backed maintenance?
4. **References** — Can they provide clients in similar industries?
5. **Pricing transparency** — Fixed-price vs. T&M, payment milestones
6. **Data security** — Especially important for government and healthcare clients
