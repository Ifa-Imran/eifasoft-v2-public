## FAQ Section

### 1. Can Eifasoft deliver projects for clients in Mumbai or Bangalore remotely?
Yes. Eifasoft serves clients across India remotely. We use video calls, Slack/Teams, JIRA, and GitHub for complete project transparency. For complex enterprise projects, we also offer on-site visits to client offices in metro cities.

### 2. Is there a cost difference based on the city?
Our pricing is based on project complexity and scope — not the client's city. A Mumbai client and a Moradabad client pay the same rate for the same project. You benefit from Tier-2 cost efficiency regardless of your location.

### 3. Does Eifasoft have physical offices in Delhi or Mumbai?
Our development and management team is based in Moradabad. We have business development representatives in Delhi and travel to Mumbai and Bangalore for project discussions when required.

### 4. What is the most in-demand software service in Tier-2 cities?
In Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities, the highest demand is for: (1) MLM software from network marketing companies, (2) coaching institute ERP, (3) basic web and mobile apps for local businesses going online, and (4) accounting software.

### 5. How quickly can Eifasoft start a project for a Lucknow or Kanpur client?
We can start a discovery call within 24 hours and begin development within 1–2 weeks of agreement signing. Location does not affect our project start timeline.
