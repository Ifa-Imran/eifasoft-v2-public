## What is Stair Step MLM Plan?

**Stair Step MLM** (also called "Breakaway Plan") is a compensation model where distributors earn commissions based on their rank, with commission percentages increasing as they advance through rank levels. When a distributor reaches the highest rank, they "break away" from their upline and become independent, earning leadership overrides from their entire organization.

### Key Characteristics:
- **Rank-Based Commissions**: Higher rank = higher commission percentage
- **Breakaway Mechanism**: Top earners become independent leaders
- **Generation Overrides**: Leaders earn from entire organization depth
- **Leadership Bonuses**: Additional rewards for top ranks
- **Team-Focused**: Rewards both personal sales and team building

### Stair Step Plan by the Numbers:

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Average Ranks** | 6-10 levels |
| **Commission Range** | 10-35% (by rank) |
| **Breakaway Threshold** | Usually top 2-3 ranks |
| **Leader Overrides** | 5-15% of organization volume |
| **Retention Rate** | 82-90% (high due to rank progression) |
| **ROI** | 175-225% within 18 months |
| **Implementation Time** | 10-14 weeks |

**Real-World Example**: A health & wellness MLM with 30,000 distributors using stair step plan generates $4.2M in monthly revenue with distributors advancing through 8 ranks on average, top 5% earning $8,000+/month from leadership overrides.

## How Breakaway Mechanics Work

### Breakaway Concept

When a distributor reaches a top rank (e.g., Diamond), they "break away" from their immediate upline and become an independent leader. Their upline then earns a smaller **leadership override** instead of full commissions.

```
Before Breakaway:
Upline (Gold) earns 25% on Diamond's group volume

After Breakaway:
Upline (Gold) earns 10% leadership override on Diamond's group
Diamond keeps full 35% commission on their own group

Benefit:
- Diamond: More earnings from their organization
- Upline: Passive income from multiple breakaway leaders
```

### Breakaway Example Timeline

```
Month 1: Join as Distributor (10% commission)
Month 3: Advance to Silver (15% commission)
Month 6: Advance to Gold (20% commission)
Month 9: Advance to Platinum (25% commission)
Month 12: Advance to Diamond (35% commission) → BREAKAWAY!

After Breakaway:
- Diamond earns 35% on own organization
- Upline (Platinum) earns 10% override on Diamond's group
- Diamond can sponsor new distributors who start at 10%
```

### Why Breakaway Works

**For Top Performers:**
- Higher commission rates (30-35%)
- Independence from upline
- Leadership override income
- Prestige and recognition

**For Upline Leaders:**
- Passive income from multiple breakaways
- No limit on breakaway leaders
- Scales infinitely
- Rewards mentorship

## Rank Advancement System

### Standard Rank Structure

| Rank | Personal Volume | Group Volume | Active Team | Commission | Timeline |
|------|----------------|--------------|-------------|------------|----------|
| **Distributor** | $100 | $0 | 0 | 10% | Starting |
| **Silver** | $200 | $1,000 | 3 | 15% | 1-3 months |

| **Crown** | $1,500 | $100,000 | 80 | 35% + bonus | 18-24 months |

### Rank Advancement Requirements

**1. Personal Volume (PV)**
- Monthly product purchases
- Demonstrates personal commitment
- Typical: $100-$1,500/month

**2. Group Volume (GV)**
- Total team sales volume
- Shows leadership ability
- Typical: $1,000-$100,000+/month

**3. Active Team Size**
- Number of active distributors
- Prevents "one-person teams"
- Typical: 3-80+ active members

**4. Consecutive Months**
- Maintain requirements for 3-6 months
- Prevents "flash ranks"
- Ensures sustainable growth

### Rank Demotion Rules

**Protective Measures:**
- **Grace Period**: 1-2 months below requirements before demotion
- **Step-Down**: Demote one rank at a time (not all the way)
- **Re-qualification**: Easy path back to previous rank
- **Legacy Protection**: Keep override on breakaways even if demoted

## Commission Structure & Formulas

### Commission by Rank

**Personal Commission (on own sales):**
- Distributor: 10%
- Silver: 15%
- Gold: 20%
- Platinum: 25%
- Diamond+: 30-35%

**Group Commission (on team sales):**
Earn the difference between your rank and downline rank.

```
Example:
You (Gold, 20%) have Silver (15%) in your team
Your commission: 20% - 15% = 5% on Silver's group volume

If Silver's group does $5,000 PV:
Your earnings: 5% × $5,000 = $250
```

### Commission Calculation Formula

```
Monthly Commission = Σ [(Your Rank % - Downline Rank %) × Downline Group PV]

Example (Gold Distributor):
- Silver 1 (15%): 5% × $3,000 GV = $150
- Silver 2 (15%): 5% × $4,000 GV = $200
- Distributor (10%): 10% × $2,000 GV = $200
- Platinum (25%): 0% (higher rank, no commission)
Total Monthly Commission: $550
```

### Breakaway Override Formula

After breakaway, upline earns override:

```
Leadership Override = Override % × Breakaway Leader's Group PV

Example:
- You (Platinum) have breakaway Diamond in your organization
- Override rate: 10%
- Diamond's group PV: $50,000
- Your override: 10% × $50,000 = $5,000/month
```

**How It Works:**
- Company allocates 2-5% of total revenue to leadership pool

**Example Pool Distribution:**
```

Qualified Leaders:
- 10 Crown (500 points each): 5,000 points total
- 30 Blue Diamond (300 points each): 9,000 points total
- 80 Diamond (100 points each): 8,000 points total

Value Per Point: $100,000 / 22,000 = $4.55

Payouts:
- Crown: 500 × $4.55 = $2,275/month
- Blue Diamond: 300 × $4.55 = $1,365/month
- Diamond: 100 × $4.55 = $455/month
```

**Solution:** Keep to 6-8 ranks for clarity.  
**Impact:** 50% faster rank comprehension.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a stair step breakaway plan?
A stair step breakaway plan is an MLM compensation model where distributors earn increasing commission percentages as they advance through ranks. When they reach top ranks, they "break away" from their upline and become independent leaders, earning higher commissions while their upline receives smaller leadership overrides. This creates motivation for rank advancement and rewards both personal sales and team building.

### How does breakaway work in MLM?
Breakaway occurs when a distributor reaches a top rank (e.g., Diamond). At that point:
- They earn full commission (30-35%) on their organization
- Their upline earns a smaller override (10-15%) instead of full commission
- They become independent leaders
- Upline can have multiple breakaway leaders earning passive income
- Breakaway motivates distributors to build large, productive teams

### What's the difference between stair step and binary plans?
**Stair Step**: Rank-based commissions, breakaway mechanism, generation overrides, focuses on team development and leadership progression.

**Binary**: Two-leg structure, spillover mechanics, pair matching bonuses, focuses on team balancing and rapid recruitment.

Stair step rewards long-term team building; binary rewards fast team growth and balancing.

### How much can you earn with stair step MLM?
Earnings vary by rank and team size:
- **Distributor**: $100-$500/month (10% commission)
- **Silver**: $300-$1,000/month (15% commission)
- **Gold**: $800-$2,500/month (20% commission)
- **Platinum**: $2,000-$5,000/month (25% commission)
- **Diamond**: $4,000-$10,000/month (30% commission + overrides)
- **Crown**: $8,000-$20,000+/month (35% commission + multiple overrides + pool bonuses)

Top 5% of distributors typically earn $5,000+/month.

### How long does it take to advance ranks?
Average rank advancement timeline:
- **Distributor → Silver**: 1-3 months
- **Silver → Gold**: 3-6 months
- **Gold → Platinum**: 6-9 months
- **Platinum → Diamond**: 9-12 months
- **Diamond → Crown**: 18-24 months

With strong training and support, 30-40% reach Gold+ within 12 months.

### What happens if I get demoted in rank?
Rank demotion typically includes protections:
- **Grace Period**: 1-2 months below requirements before demotion
- **Step-Down**: Demote one rank at a time (not all the way)
- **Legacy Protection**: Keep override on previous breakaways
- **Easy Re-qualification**: Return to previous rank by meeting requirements for 1 month

### How does leadership pool bonus work?
Leadership pool bonus:
- Company allocates 2-5% of total revenue to pool
- Distributed among top ranks (Diamond+)
- Proportional to rank points (higher rank = more points)
- Paid monthly in addition to regular commissions
- Example: $100,000 pool split among 120 leaders = $833 average bonus

### Is stair step MLM legal?
Yes, stair step MLM is legal when properly structured:
- Focus on product sales, not recruitment
- Publish income disclosure statements
- Offer refund policy
- Comply with FTC guidelines
- Register where required
- Consult MLM attorney before launch
